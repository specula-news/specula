  • V23.0.0 Nov 2026 · Current
    Ember & Static Hosting:
    • New look “Ember”: Warm near-black with amber, coral and green signals, Fraunces display type, sidebar navigation with icons and new-story counts, segmented Articles/Videos tabs with counts, list view with freshness bars. The V22 look stays available as Classic.
